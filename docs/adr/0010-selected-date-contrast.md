# ADR 0010: Selected dates are ink on accent

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Owner:** container
- **Replaces:** `selected-date-contrast` (former decision entries)

## Context

The mockup draws selected dates in white on `accent`, 3.3:1, and the brief requires WCAG AA for every text pair.

## Decision

Selected dates carry their text in `ink` on `accent` (5.3:1).

## Consequences

Following the mockup's white fails WCAG AA.
