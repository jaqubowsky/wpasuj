# ADR 0021: The app follows the v3 boards

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Owner:** owner; container (`poll-page-columns`, `desktop-meta-line`, `check-icon-inline`)
- **Replaces:** `v3-look`, `poll-page-columns`, `check-icon-inline`, `desktop-meta-line` (former decision entries)

## Context

`spec/design/v2/` holds the mockups the brief names, and `AGENTS.md` compares screens with them. The owner then approved a new look for every app screen (canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/65gibAJh1194kmFL6BobRn; copies in the host's `host-acceptance/design-v3/`, outside the repository).

## Decision

- Where the v3 boards and `spec/design/v2/` differ, v3 wins: one person row with state on the avatar, one grid look for both tabs, fixed desktop columns in both tabs, an organiser card with a "⋯" sheet or menu, no duplicated counts, "Nie mogę" at the top of the Moje card (WPA-42, WPA-43)
- From 1024px the poll page is two fixed columns in both tabs (grid `minmax(0,1fr) 340px`, `PageFrame wide` 1120px): head full width, then the tab switch with its body on the left and "Najlepiej teraz", "Twoja ankieta", the people card on the right, top-aligned with the tab switch; the name field sits under the tab switch there and above it on the phone. Date headers read "pt 17" at every width. The gone page links "Zrób własną ankietę". "Też dobre", the ramp legend, "Kto odpowiedział" times, "Gotowe. Zmieniasz zdanie?" and the "Nie możesz…" lines under the grid are gone with the boards; "Ustal termin" sets the best time. A tapped hour opens a sheet on the phone and replaces "Odpowiedzieli" in the people card on desktop (WPA-42)
- From 1024px the poll header's meta line reads only "Kuba pyta" (or "Pytasz jako Kuba"), with no date range, and "Kliknij godzinę, żeby zobaczyć, kto może." sits above the heatmap (boards DesktopWszyscy, Organiser), replacing `results-desktop.html`'s meta line and hint (WPA-21)
- The check icon stays inline at the three sizes the boards draw, not the brief's one Lucide size: 14px with a 2.5 stroke in the "Ustalone" badge, 20px with 2.5 on the invite card's coral disc, 18px with 1.5 beside "Nie mogę"; a shared icon would take a size and a stroke per caller (WPA-21, note from PR #34)

## Consequences

- A screen compared only with `spec/design/v2/` can be "fixed" back to the old look; `spec/design/v2/results-desktop.html` no longer describes the desktop poll page
- The v3 boards are not in the repository, so a disagreement with them goes to the host
