# ADR 0029: Heat cells carry no result marks

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Owner:** owner
- **Replaces:** `no-result-marks` (former decision entries)

## Context

The brief and the mockups ringed the cells where everyone is free and outlined the best time.

## Decision

Heatmap cells carry no mark beyond their heat and count: the ink ring where everyone is free, the ink outline on the best time or the set time's hours, and the ring on story scene 6's best cells are gone; the best-time card alone names the best time. The owner turned down a coral frame around the run, an ink bar beside it and an ink corner on its hours (WPA-53).

## Consequences

Restoring a mark from the brief or a mockup undoes an owner decision.
