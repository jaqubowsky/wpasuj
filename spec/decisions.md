# Decisions

Points `spec/brief.md` leaves open, taken by the host. A container that meets another open point takes the simpler option and adds an entry here in its pull request.

Each entry reads `slug` (who, date): decision. Who is the owner, the host, or the container that took the simpler option in its pull request; the date is the day the entry was recorded. Other files name an entry by its slug, and work items by their WPA key in Linear.
