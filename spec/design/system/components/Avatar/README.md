# Avatar

A respondent's initial on a soft tint in a rounded square (`radius-cell`): the only person marker; there are no photos.

- The consumer provides the name; the tint is picked from the normalised name, so a person keeps their colour.
- `data-pop` animates a respondent who just arrived live (`duration-pop`, `ease-pop`), the one overshoot in the product.
- `stack` sets it in an overlapping row of faces on a ground (`coral`, `ink` or `paper`): 40px with a 12px radius, 8px overlap and a 3px ring of the ground's colour, as in the poll poster's count (ADR 0035).
- The organiser's crown and the cross of someone who cannot stay round badges.
