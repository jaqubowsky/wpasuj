# SiteLinks

The footer's way to the site's information: "Polityka prywatności", "Regulamin" and "Kontakt" (`mailto:kontakt@wpasuj.pl`), three `TextLink`s in a `nav` named "Informacje".

- They wrap and centre, 24px apart; on the phone they may break onto two lines.
- `tone="on-dark"` passes to every `TextLink`, for the landing's `ink` footer; the poll page and the legal pages use the default `ink` text on `paper`.
- It sits in the landing footer, the poll page footer and the legal pages' footer. The consumer places it; it takes no words.
